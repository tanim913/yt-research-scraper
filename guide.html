Start the app

Questions

The scraper runs on your own computer. Your keys and data never leave it.

  1. Install Node.js 20 or newer from nodejs.org (the “LTS” button). Nothing else needs installing.
  2. Open a terminal in the scraper’s folder and run:
node server.js
  1. Open http://127.0.0.1:3000 in your browser. Keep the terminal open while you work; press Ctrl + C there to stop.
Stopping is safe. Jobs are saved after every page of results and carry on when you start the app again. Everything lives in the data/ folder; don’t share it, because it contains your API keys.
2

Get API keys

Questions

The scraper uses YouTube’s official API. Each key is free and gives 10,000 units a day.

This is not an AI key. You need a YouTube Data API v3 key from Google Cloud. Keys from ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini won’t work, and no AI is used anywhere in the scraper.
  1. Go to console.cloud.google.com, sign in with any Google account, and create a project.
  2. Open APIs & Services → Library, search for YouTube Data API v3, and press Enable.
  3. Open Credentials → Create credentials → API key, and copy the key (it starts with AIza).
  4. In the app, paste it into step 1 · API keys and press Add key.
More keys only help if they come from different projects. Keys inside one project share that project’s 10,000 units. For extra quota, repeat the steps with a new project (teammates can each make one).

How much quota do I need?

Each unit is one request to YouTube. Rough costs:

What you doCost
Find videos from channels~1 unit per 25 videos
Search all of YouTube100 units per 50 results
Scrape comments~1 unit per 80–100 comments
Changing research rulesFree

For example, about 86,000 comments from three news channels is roughly 1,000 units for the comments plus a few hundred to scan the channels, which fits inside one key’s day. Search mode is where quota disappears fast.

3

Find videos

Questions

Step 2 in the app decides where the scraper looks. Pick one of two ways.

From channels (recommended)

List the channels you study, one per line. Any of these forms work:

@BBCNews https://www.youtube.com/@aljazeeraenglish https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC16niRr50-MSBwiO3YDb3RA

Set Published from and until to your study period. The scraper reads each channel’s uploads from newest to oldest and stops once it passes your start date, which keeps it cheap.

Search YouTube

Type search words, for example russia ukraine war. Use this when you don’t know which channels matter. It costs 100 units per 50 results, and the app shows the cost before you start. YouTube returns at most about 500 results per search.

While it runs

Name the collection (so you can find it later) and press Find videos. Frames appear on the contact sheet as videos arrive; you can already adjust your rules while you wait.

4

Research rules

Questions

Rules decide which of the found videos stay on the sheet. They run on your computer, so changing them is instant and free. Leave a box empty to switch that rule off.

RuleWhat it does
Must mentionKeeps videos whose title, description or tags contain your words. Separate words with commas. Choose at least one word or every word.
Must not mentionRemoves videos that contain any of these words.
Views, comments, likesMinimum and maximum. For example, at least 100 comments.
Discussion levelComments per 1,000 views. Finds videos people actually argued about.
LengthIn minutes.
LanguageSuch as en or bn. Only removes videos whose uploader set a different language; videos with no language set are kept.
CategoryFor example News & Politics.
Skip ShortsRemoves videos of 60 seconds or less, and any tagged #shorts.
Skip comments offRemoves videos where comments are turned off. They have nothing to scrape.
Skip live streams, kids’ videosRemoves live or scheduled streams, and videos marked made for kids.

Save your rule set under Saved rule sets so you can reuse exactly the same rules later, and quote them in your methods section. Rule sets are saved in this browser.

5

Review and scrape

Questions

Every video that passes your rules appears as a frame, circled in red. Circled frames get scraped.

  • Click a frame to cross it out. Click again to circle it back. Click the title to open the video on YouTube.
  • Sort by date, comments, views or length; Circle all and Clear all act on every matching video.
  • videos.csv saves the list of matching videos with their numbers, which is useful for your dataset table.

The strip at the bottom

It always shows what scraping your circled videos will cost against what’s left today.

Before pressing Scrape comments, choose:

  • Order: Top comments (YouTube’s relevance order) or Newest.
  • Max per video: leave it empty to get all comments, or set a cap such as 500 to keep big videos from dominating.
  • All replies: YouTube only includes up to 5 replies per comment. Tick this to fetch every reply (costs more).
6

Jobs and quota

Questions

Every search and every scrape is a job in the Jobs list. Jobs run one at a time, in order.

StatusMeaning
WaitingIn line behind another job.
RunningWorking now. For scrapes you can download the CSV already.
PausedEvery key ran out of quota. It continues by itself after the reset, or as soon as you add a key from another project.
DoneFinished. Open it (search) or download comments.csv (scrape).
ErrorSomething failed, for example the internet dropped. Press Resume to try again from where it stopped.
CancelledYou stopped it. Resume is still possible.

Reading the quota

The black strip at the top of the app shows today’s quota as 20 frames. Lit frames are what’s left across all your keys.

Quota resets at midnight Pacific time; the app shows that moment in your own time (around 1–2 PM in Bangladesh). Nothing is lost when quota runs out: the job saves its place and continues.

The CSV files

Questions

Both files open in Excel, Google Sheets, or Python. Bangla, Russian and other scripts display correctly.

comments.csv

One row per comment or reply. Download it from Jobs.

ColumnContains
video_id, video_title, channelWhich video the comment is from.
comment_idYouTube’s ID for the comment.
parent_id, is_replyFor replies: is_reply is 1 and parent_id is the comment it answers. Empty and 0 for top-level comments.
author, textDisplay name and the comment text exactly as written.
like_count, published_atLikes, and the time posted (UTC).
reply_countHow many replies a top-level comment has.

videos.csv

video_id, url, title, channel, published_at, views, likes, comments, duration_s, language, tags. Numbers are as they were when the videos were found.

Open it in Python

import pandas as pd df = pd.read_csv("comments.csv") top_level = df[df.is_reply == 0]
Excel tip: a comment that starts with =, +, - or @ may be read as a formula. For analysis, use Python or Google Sheets’ import, which keep the text as it is.
8

Troubleshooting

Questions

Find what you see, then open it.

A key says “Invalid key. Check it was copied fully”

The key was cut off or mistyped. Copy it again from Credentials in Google Cloud, remove the old one with the bin button, and add it again.

A key says “YouTube Data API v3 is not enabled”

The key’s project hasn’t enabled the API. Open APIs & Services → Library in that project, enable YouTube Data API v3, then remove and re-add the key.

The Jobs list says “Channel not found”

Check the handle on the channel’s page; it’s the part after @ in the link. Old links like youtube.com/c/Name sometimes don’t match a handle; open the channel and copy the link from the address bar instead.

A video was “skipped: comments are off”

The uploader turned comments off, so there is nothing to collect. The job moves on to the next video. Keep Skip comments off ticked to avoid picking these.

Everything is paused / “All keys are out of quota”

Today’s quota is used up. Leave the app running and it continues after the reset, or add a key from another Google Cloud project to continue right away.

No videos match my rules

Loosen one rule at a time; it costs nothing. Common culprits: Must mention set to every word, a high minimum comment count, or a language code when the channel doesn’t set one.

The terminal says the port is already in use

The app is probably already running in another terminal; open 127.0.0.1:3000. Or start it on another port:

PORT=4000 node server.js
An old video I expected is missing from a big channel

YouTube only lists about the latest 20,000 uploads of a channel. For older videos on very large channels, use Search YouTube with a date range for that period.

The numbers differ from what YouTube shows now

Views and comment counts are recorded when the videos were found. Run Find videos again for fresh numbers, and note the collection date in your paper.

Your methods section

Questions

Reviewers will ask how videos were chosen. Your saved rule set is the answer. Here is a template to adapt; replace the red parts.

Comments were collected through the YouTube Data API v3. Videos were drawn from the uploads of [channels, e.g. BBC News, CNN and Al Jazeera English] published between [start date] and [end date]. We kept videos whose title, description or tags mentioned [keywords], with at least [N] comments, excluding Shorts (60 seconds or less) and videos with comments disabled. [N] of [N] candidate videos met these criteria after manual review, yielding [N] comments and replies collected on [collection date].
